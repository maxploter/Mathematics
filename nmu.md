# НМУ (Независимый московский университет) — reference

Facts gathered 2026-08-29/30 while deciding to study there. Keep this file updated as things are
confirmed or change — don't re-research from scratch.

## What it is

Founded 1991 by a group of well-known mathematicians. Non-state, no state accreditation, issues
no state-recognized diploma. No entrance exam, no formal enrollment: «Ни зачисления, ни
отчисления в НМУ нет.» Courses are open and free.

Classes are evening (usually from 17:30) specifically because most students combine НМУ with
another university or a job elsewhere.

## The decision (autumn 2026)

**Матанализ-1** (Шапошников) + **Алгебра-1** (Авилов), as вольнослушатель, remote via
recordings. Skip Геометрия. Not chasing слушатель status this year. Targeting the **February**
exam window, not December.

Why: matanalysis is the prerequisite spine (needed before Теория вероятностей, the course
originally wanted); Алгебра-1 already contains linear algebra in the Russian tradition — vector
spaces, Gauss, rank, determinants, eigenvectors, Jordan/Frobenius normal forms are all in
Авилов's programme, so it's not a separate course to add later. Геометрия is the one fall course
with no spring continuation, so it's the cheapest to drop — costs one year on Дифференциальная
геометрия, nothing else.

## Study length and status ladder

«Обучение в НМУ, как правило, длится 5 лет» (elastic — can shorten or extend). Years 1–2: fixed
core (Анализ, Алгебра, Геометрия, Комплексный анализ, Топология, Теория вероятностей). Years
3–5: спецкурсы (changes each semester) + дипломная работа.

Two statuses: **вольнослушатель** (default, no application) and **слушатель** (carved out by
results). Вольнослушатель rights: attend freely, «сдавать экзамены и зачеты», library, printing.
Results recorded in the протоколы either way.

Слушатель of semester k (1 < k < 10) requires: ≥1 course passed the previous semester, AND
≥ (3k−4) courses passed in total. This is calibrated to exactly 3 courses/semester (one course
of slack). At 2/semester the count (2n) falls below the bar (3n−1) starting at k=3 — status
lapses. Formula only applies for k in (1,10); beyond that the only basis for status is
«дипломный год».

«Слушателям, сдавшим экзамены **очно**, назначается специальная премия на один семестр» — the
only place presence is rewarded, and it's money, not eligibility. No residency/attendance
condition anywhere else in the rules.

## Licentiate and diploma (rules effective 01.01.2026)

Licentiate (диплом лиценциата) is now a **mandatory** step toward the full НМУ diploma. 15-course
list:
1. Математический анализ-1, 2. Математический анализ-2, 3. Анализ на многообразиях,
4. Алгебра-1, 5. Алгебра-2, 6. Алгебра-3, 7. Геометрия, 8. Топология-1, 9. Топология-2,
10. Дифференциальная геометрия, 11. Алгебра-4, 12. Топология-3, 13. Теория вероятностей,
14. Комплексный анализ, 15. Дифференциальные уравнения.

Need 12 of 15, **including all of the first 10**. Stated as pure count, no status condition —
reachable as вольнослушатель. Full diploma needs ≥24 exams + licentiate, and is written for a
«Слушатель» — likely requires status, unconfirmed.

Дипломник: declare intent by 15 March, thesis text in by 1 May, defence late May–early June
(exceptionally Sept–Oct). Needs written recommendation from a научный руководитель — nothing in
the rules requires that supervision be in-person.

**Transfer credit:** up to 5 courses can be credited from another university by decision of a
commission — untested lever, would materially help the status arithmetic if usable against the
MSc.

## Remote study — from the "НМУ community | 2026" Telegram group (2 yrs history, 58k msgs)

Group: `t.me/+lP-q4pwxiD80NjZi`, ~717 members. Joined 2026-08-30.

- Fully remote is possible but lecturer-dependent: no site-wide guarantee, arranged per course.
- **Inverted burden:** «зачёт является допуском только к дистанционному экзамену, для очного
  экзамена не надо никакого допуска» — remote students need листки done first; in-person
  students don't.
- **No per-sheet deadlines** for remote submission — any order, due «до повторного экзамена»,
  retakes allowed.
- Remote exams are real (Google Meet, camera+mic, no internet allowed) but got pulled for
  Анализ in Feb 2025 after irregularities — can be revoked.
- Remote graders (принимающие) are scarce, arranged 1:1 — one grader for topology got ~100
  requests. **Action: ask in the group who's accepting Матанализ-1 / Алгебра-1 листки remotely
  this semester, before 9 Sept.**
- Missed courses roll to next year; passed courses persist regardless of status.
- Шапошников: consensus best analysis lecturer («лектор от Бога», specialises in analysis).
